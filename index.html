About ChillPod

Smart cooling pads

Gel packs warm up fast and need a freezer. ChillPod uses built-in cooling elements to hold a steady, safe temperature between 10°C and 18°C.

Set your temperature and timer right from our mobile app. Our latest design moves the battery into a pocket-sized wireless charging case, leaving only light magnetic cooling pads on your temples.
