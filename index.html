A cooler approach to headache relief

Meet ChillPod.

Portable. Wearable. Adjustable.

ChillPod is a wearable device under development that applies localized cold therapy to the temples. Our goal is to make cooling-based support for stress-induced headaches and mild migraines more convenient at home, at work, or while travelling.

About ChillPod

Cold therapy, rethought.

Headaches and migraines can interfere with everyday life. Cold therapy is a non-invasive approach commonly used to help manage headache discomfort. However, traditional solutions such as ice packs and cooling caps may need to be cooled again, can be bulky, and generally do not let users actively adjust the temperature.

ChillPod explores a different approach: a lightweight, portable electronic device designed to deliver controlled cooling directly to the temple area. By combining active thermoelectric cooling with a smartphone app, we aim to give users more control over their cooling sessions.

ChillPod is currently in the technical development stage. Further optimization and testing are needed before its performance and potential benefits can be established.

How It Works

Active Cooling

Two Peltier elements use the thermoelectric effect to create cooling at the temples. Temperature sensors provide feedback so the system can regulate the cooling level.

Intelligent Control

An ESP32-based control unit manages the cooling elements. It uses a control signal to adjust their current and a feedback loop to help maintain the selected temperature.

App Connectivity

A planned mobile app connects to ChillPod via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE). It is designed to let users adjust temperature and treatment duration and review session information. Android and iOS are the intended platforms.

Our Journey

2nd Place at COSIMA 2025

ChillPod received second place at COSIMA 2025. This recognition encouraged the team to continue developing the concept and improve the first prototype.

Prototype 1.0

The first prototype demonstrated the cooling concept using a headband with two Peltier modules. During testing, it maintained a temperature of around 10°C for approximately 6 minutes with skin contact and 10 minutes without. Integrating all components into the headband also made the device heavier and more difficult to troubleshoot.

Designing Version 2.0

The next concept focuses on comfort and mobility. The electronics and battery are moved into a separate handheld case, while a lighter headband holds the cooling pads in place. Wireless charging is also part of the proposed design.
