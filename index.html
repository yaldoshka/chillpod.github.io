About ChillPod

Smart Thermoelectric Cooling Pads

Unlike passive gel packs or ice wraps that warm up quickly and need constant refrigeration, ChillPod uses active Peltier thermoelectric elements with a smart sensor feedback loop to maintain a steady, clinically recommended temperature between 10°C and 18°C.

Controlled via Bluetooth (BLE) through our Android and iOS mobile app, users can customize temperatures, keep sessions within the safe 15-minute medical window, and share treatment data with healthcare professionals. Our Version 2.0 concept separates the electronics and wireless-charging battery case from a lightweight headband with magnetic temple holders for maximum comfort.
